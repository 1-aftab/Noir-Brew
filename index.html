A room made for lingering

Noir & Brew opened with one grinder, six tables and a belief that good coffee deserves a quiet room. Ten years on, the grinder has company, but the pace has stayed the same.

We roast in small batches with a local roaster, bake every morning and cook a short menu that changes with the season. Come for a flat white, stay for supper.

  • Small-batch beans, roasted weekly
  • Baked daily in our own kitchen
  • Open late on Friday and Saturday

What we're known for

The house roast

A chocolate-and-orange blend roasted in small batches, ground fresh for every cup.

One long table, three courses and a menu we only decide on Saturday night.
